# The periods appear unreported; priority is not proved

The catalogue and literature audit completed on **3 October 2026 (India)** found no earlier report of either measured period. The stars themselves, the first star's magnetism, and published atmosphere-dependent masses were already known.

## The qualification that matters most

SDSS J112148.80+103934.2 matches **ATO J170.4531+10.6594** within 0.068 arcseconds in the [ATLAS variable-star catalogue](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/J/AJ/156/241). Its class is `dubious`. Its algorithmic period fields contain 3.380501, 21.167702 and 1.540327 days; the final short-period Fourier-fit field is missing. None is the 117.4-minute cycle reported here.

The earlier flag must be credited. The new contribution appears to be establishing this cycle and characterising its spectral changes.

[Kepler et al. (2016)](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/455/4/3413/1264189), Figure 4, already showed its magnetic spectrum under **SDSS J112148.77+103934.1**, with a field near 309 MG. [Amorim et al. (2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.08862) and [Hardy, Dufour & Jordan (2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.06605) provide later magnetic/atmosphere context. This repository does not claim that their results are new.

## What was checked

- Exact Gaia DR3 IDs **3915674026806527616** and **1974721783974773376**, coordinate names and the first star's alternate SDSS spelling.
- SIMBAD aliases and linked bibliography; live VSX and its VizieR copy; Gaia DR3 variability classifier, short-timescale and rotation tables.
- ATLAS, Catalina periodic variables, and published ZTF variable/suspected-variable catalogues.
- Coordinate matching to the full downloaded Lin and Jestin tables, plus targeted recent white-dwarf period literature and object-specific papers.

No VSX entry was found within 60 arcseconds of either star. The other general-variable checks found no matching entry except the first star's ATLAS flag. Selection limits matter: a short-period catalogue's absence says little about a 3.431-day source.

The [query manifests and snapshots](../provenance/) preserve the searches, response hashes, matches and empty responses. They distinguish failed queries from valid empty results. Direct ADS full-text pages could not be retrieved; that is a gap, not a negative result. Unindexed tables, other aliases and unpublished work remain possible.

These are the appropriate current claims: **“apparently unreported period”** and **“candidate”** where confirmation is incomplete. A public GitHub repository makes the work inspectable; it does not certify correctness or priority.
